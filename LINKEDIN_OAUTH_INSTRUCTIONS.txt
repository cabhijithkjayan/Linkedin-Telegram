LinkedIn OAuth setup instructions:

1. Log in to LinkedIn Developer Portal.
2. Create or open an app.
3. Add the redirect URI exactly as used by the app, for example:
   http://localhost:8000/callback
4. Request the OAuth scopes:
   openid profile email w_member_social
5. Copy the Client ID and Client Secret to your environment or GitHub secrets.
6. Use the access token flow or Authorized OAuth code flow to generate a valid token.
7. The app needs permission to post to your own account.

Important:
- Redirect URIs must match exactly.
- Use a real app credential, not a fake value.
- Keep your secrets out of source control.
